# Fast Food Industry Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes the U.S. fast-food industry using 2022 sales, restaurant-unit, and growth data from leading restaurant companies. The analysis uses Tableau to compare company performance, average sales per restaurant, changes in sales and restaurant units, and restaurant distribution across cuisine types.

The project was completed in Tableau as part of the Global Career Accelerator Data Analytics program.

## Business Question
How do leading fast-food companies compare in sales performance, restaurant footprint, and year-over-year growth, and how does performance vary across cuisine types?

## Data
The analysis uses a fast-food company directory containing information on leading restaurant companies and their U.S. operations.

Key variables include:
- Company
- Rank
- Cuisine Type
- Total 2022 U.S. Sales
- Total 2021 U.S. Sales
- 1-Year Change in U.S. Sales
- Total 2022 U.S. Units
- Total 2021 U.S. Units
- 1-Year Change in U.S. Units
- 2022 Average Unit Volume
- Company-owned units
- Franchised units
- International units

The analysis focuses on the top 20 ranked companies in the dataset.

## Analysis
I created Tableau visualizations to compare financial performance, restaurant scale, and year-over-year changes across leading fast-food companies.

Key analysis included:
- Comparing total U.S. sales by company
- Comparing average sales per restaurant
- Examining year-over-year changes in U.S. sales and restaurant units
- Comparing restaurant presence across different cuisine types
- Identifying the top three companies by rank
- Using Tableau calculated fields to isolate the top three companies
- Using aggregated measures to compare company performance
- Comparing sales performance with restaurant footprint to provide additional context

## Key Findings
### Total U.S. Sales
The analysis shows substantial differences in U.S. sales among the top 20 companies.

McDonald's had the highest reported 2022 U.S. sales in the dataset at approximately $48.7 billion, followed by Starbucks at $28.1 billion and Chick-fil-A at $18.8 billion.

### Average Sales per Restaurant
Average sales per restaurant varied considerably across companies.

Chick-fil-A had the highest reported 2022 average unit volume among the top 20 companies at approximately $6.83 million per unit. Other companies with relatively high average unit volumes included Olive Garden ($5.33 million), McDonald's ($3.63 million), and Panera Bread ($2.90 million).

This comparison provides additional context beyond total sales because companies operate with substantially different numbers of restaurants.

### Changes in Sales and Restaurant Units
Year-over-year changes in U.S. sales and restaurant units varied across companies.

For example, Panda Express recorded a 16.8% increase in U.S. sales, while Chipotle Mexican Grill increased sales by 14.8%. In contrast, Sonic Drive-In experienced a 5.8% decrease in U.S. sales.

Comparing sales growth alongside changes in restaurant units provides a way to distinguish changes in overall sales from changes in restaurant footprint.

### Restaurant Footprint by Cuisine
The analysis also compares the number of U.S. restaurant units across cuisine types. Burger, coffee/cafe, sandwich, chicken, Mexican, and pizza companies represent substantial portions of the restaurant footprint among the companies analyzed.
