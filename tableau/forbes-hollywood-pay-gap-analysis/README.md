# Forbes and the Pay Gap in Hollywood

## Overview
This project analyzes Forbes celebrity earnings data to examine differences in reported earnings between male and female actors in Hollywood from 2005–2020. The analysis uses Tableau to compare average earnings, track changes in earnings over time, and identify the highest-paid actors within the dataset.

The project was completed in Tableau as part of the Global Career Accelerator Data Analytics program.

## Business Question
How do reported earnings differ between male and female actors, and how have these differences changed over time?

## Data
The analysis uses Forbes celebrity earnings data from 2005–2020.

Key variables include:
- Name
- Gender
- Pay (USD millions)
- Year
- Category

The dataset records reported celebrity earnings and allows comparisons across gender and year.

## Analysis
I created Tableau visualizations to examine actor earnings across gender and time.

Key analysis included:
- Comparing average earnings between male and female actors
- Examining actor earnings over time from 2005–2020
- Comparing yearly earnings by gender
- Identifying the highest-paid actors in the dataset
- Examining the distribution of reported earnings across actors
- Using Tableau aggregations to calculate and compare earnings by gender and year

## Key Findings
### Earnings by Gender
The analysis shows differences in average reported earnings between male and female actors in the dataset. Comparing average pay by gender provides an overview of the earnings gap represented in the Forbes data.

### Earnings Over Time
Actor earnings varied substantially from year to year between 2005 and 2020. Examining the data over time makes it possible to identify periods when the difference between male and female earnings was larger or smaller.

### Highest-Paid Actors
The highest-paid-actor visualization highlights the individuals with the largest reported earnings in the dataset and provides additional context for the overall earnings distributions.

### Important Context
These findings describe reported Forbes earnings within this particular dataset. They should not be interpreted as representing the earnings of all actors or the entire Hollywood industry, since the dataset focuses on celebrities included in the Forbes rankings.
