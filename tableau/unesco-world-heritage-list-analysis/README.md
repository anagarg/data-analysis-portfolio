# Exploring the UNESCO World Heritage List

## Overview
This project analyzes UNESCO World Heritage List data to explore the geographic distribution, classification, and endangered status of World Heritage Sites around the world. The analysis uses Tableau to compare the number of heritage sites and the amount of land they cover across countries and categories.

The project was completed in Tableau as part of the Global Career Accelerator Data Analytics program.

## Business Question
How are World Heritage Sites distributed across countries and categories, and what patterns can be observed among sites classified as endangered?

## Data
The analysis uses data from the UNESCO World Heritage List.

Key variables include:
- Country
- World Heritage Site
- Category
- Danger List status
- Area (hectares)

The data allows World Heritage Sites to be compared based on their geographic location, classification, land area, and endangered status.

## Analysis
I created Tableau visualizations to explore the distribution and characteristics of World Heritage Sites.

Key analysis included:
- Comparing the number of World Heritage Sites by country
- Comparing the total heritage-site area by country
- Examining the distribution of sites across different heritage categories
- Comparing endangered sites across categories
- Comparing endangered and non-endangered sites within each category
- Using geographic and categorical visualizations to identify patterns in the World Heritage List

## Key Findings
### Heritage Sites by Country
The number of World Heritage Sites varies substantially across countries. The country-level visualization highlights geographic differences in the concentration of recognized World Heritage Sites.

### Heritage Area
Countries also differ in the total area represented by their World Heritage Sites. Comparing site counts with total area provides a different perspective on the geographic scale of World Heritage protection.

### Sites by Category
The World Heritage List contains multiple categories of sites. Comparing the number of sites within each category shows how the sites in the dataset are distributed across these classifications.

### Endangered Sites
The analysis compares endangered and non-endangered sites across World Heritage categories. This provides insight into how endangered-status classifications are distributed among the different categories of sites.
